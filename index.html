01 / 07 Capstone · 2025–26

Seawater electrolyzer & degradation model

A pilot alkaline electrolyzer that makes hydrogen from Red Sea water, with a Simulink model that predicts how fast its electrodes wear.

Seawater destroys ordinary electrodes: chloride ions pit the anode within hours. Our four-person team coated a stainless-steel mesh anode with a NiFe-phosphate layer and built a laser-cut test cell to compare it against a bare electrode.

I built the model. A Simscape electrolyzer (electrical, thermal-liquid and gas networks) feeds current, voltage and temperature into three Arrhenius degradation pathways: kinetic wear, chloride pitting and oxide growth. Their combined health factor is fed back into the circuit as a rising series resistance, so the simulated cell loses output as it degrades.

  • Simscape
  • Electrochemistry
  • SolidWorks

02 / 07 Formula Student

Battery model development

Physics-based cell and pack simulation used to choose the cells for the team's second EV, extended into on-car state-of-charge estimation.

Usable cell models don't exist for most candidate cells, and none capture full-pack geometry and cooling. I extracted look-up tables from datasheets, built packs in MATLAB's Battery Builder, and drove them with throttle and brake traces recorded from an FSUK track in Assetto Corsa, reshaped into real power demand.

I then fitted 2RC equivalent-circuit parameters from virtual pulse tests at nine temperatures (20–60 °C). The resulting tables feed an Extended Kalman Filter for SOC estimation on a 96s4p NMC pack.

  • Battery Builder

03 / 07 Formula Student

Tractive-system accumulator container

The crash-rated box that holds the battery pack and its high-voltage safety systems.

The chassis had been designed before the accumulator was sized, so everything had to fit a volume that was never meant for it, and the container still had to survive crash loads. I first designed the ideal enclosure for the pack and its safety devices, then cut it down to the chassis, trading some ease of assembly and maintenance for fit while keeping a margin in case the built chassis differed from CAD.

04 / 07 Formula Student

Differential sprocket adapter

A lightweight replacement for the bulky part linking the driven sprocket to the limited-slip differential.

There was no CAD model of the sprocket, so I measured the bolt pattern by hand, modelled it, and checked it against the real part with a laser-cut cardboard template. A topology optimisation under worst-case torque (motor peak × 3.57 ratio) showed where material could go; I redrew the part for CNC machining and verified it in FEA.

05 / 07 Venture concept

Virtual power plant dispatch optimiser

Optimisation software for batteries and rooftop PV at commercial sites in Jordan, shifting evening peak load onto stored solar.

I came up with the venture idea and specified the optimiser behind it: an hourly mixed-integer linear program over 24 hours that decides every flow between grid, PV, battery and load to minimise the bill under time-of-use tariffs. It respects state-of-charge and power limits, round-trip efficiency, and never charges and discharges at once. Every run is compared with a PV-first baseline, so savings are measured rather than assumed.

AI process control for PTFE liner yield

A failure-aware, engineering-validated AI workflow to cut scrap in medical catheter-liner manufacturing.

With team Vector, I defined the AI workflow and owned its engineering validation. We modelled the process as a causal chain (process parameters → material properties → failure modes), then layered correlation, mediation and random-forest sensitivity analysis. Every significant link was checked against PTFE material science to discard statistical artefacts, leaving one controlled operating procedure.

Residential PV system

A rooftop system sized to cover a household's yearly consumption, from load analysis to bill of materials.

I logged every appliance to build the household load profile, modelled the roof and its water tanks in SketchUp, and ran shading, loss and yield analysis in PVsyst. Some rooftop equipment had to move to fit the array in its best position; loss calculations also set where the inverter went to balance DC and AC cable runs.
